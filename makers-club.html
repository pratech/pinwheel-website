What is Pinwheel Makers Club?

Pinwheel Makers Club is a weekly hands-on STEM learning program designed for students who love building, experimenting and creating. Instead of memorizing concepts, students learn Robotics, Electronics, Programming, AI and Engineering by building real working projects. Every session combines creativity, problem solving and practical engineering skills.

Why Join?

🤖 Build Real Projects

Robots, smart systems, automation projects and engineering models.

🚀 Future Ready Skills

👨‍🔬 Think Like an Engineer

Creativity, debugging, teamwork and innovation.
